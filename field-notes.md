## Cross-cutting essence (what the photos say, together)

1. **Module × variation** — a repeated unit where every instance differs slightly: ceramic room signs, handmade tiles, arch windows, balconies, hanafuda cards, blind boxes. Mass production with a hand in it.
2. **Line + arc as a complete vocabulary** — plaques, diagrams, directory, canopy, monument. Straight lines and circle segments are *enough*.
3. **Light is the animator** — night arches, stair towers, lamps in darkness, golden-hour gradients. Things don't get "highlighted"; they *ignite*.
4. **Visible care** — propped pines, displayed fire hose, preservation plaques, exposed distillation rigs. Support structures shown proudly, not hidden.
5. **Two registers** — *paper calm* (cream grounds, sparse color events, letterpress) and *market density* (sticker joy, collection grids, warm clutter). Same country, same day, both deliberate.
6. **Tactility & imperfection** — thick grout, glaze gloss, laminate sheen, letterpress bite, weathered wood, a physical SOLD OUT sticker. Surfaces have hands in their history.
7. **Hand-drawn inside systems** — illustration never floats free; it lives in boxes, arches, pills, cards.
8. **Signage as unified system** — identical white discs for eight businesses; numbered clipboards. One shape, many tenants.
